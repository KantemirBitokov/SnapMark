Everything for clear feedback

Bug reports, design reviews, instructions, support — without “the button on the left… no, the other one”.

Markers with comments

A, B, C or 1, 2, 3 — four shapes, arrows, any colors. Comments become ready-made text.

📋

One Ctrl+C for a series

Collect several screenshots and paste images and text together into Telegram, Slack, Jira, Word.

✍️

Natural drawing

Pen that reacts to speed and pressure; hold the stroke and it becomes a clean arrow or shape.

🔤

Text with style

Any font and weight, frames, plates and shadows — each object has its own style panel.

🎬

Screen recording

MP4, MKV, WebM or GIF, up to 60 fps, with microphone and computer audio. No time limit.
